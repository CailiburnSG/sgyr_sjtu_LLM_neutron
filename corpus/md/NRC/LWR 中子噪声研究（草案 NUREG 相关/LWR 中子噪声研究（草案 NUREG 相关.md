## RESEARCH HIGHLIGHTS FOR FY1983

- COMPLETED PWR PRESSURE NOISE ASSESSMENT INCLUDING MODEL OF PRESSURE NOISE
  IN PWR PRIMARY SYSTEM-
- COMPLETED SEQUOYAH DEMONSTRATION.
- INITIATED PEACH BOTTOM DEMONSTRATION.
- INITIATED AUTOMATED DIAGNOSTIC STUDY.
- ASSESSED LOOSE PARTS MONITORING.
- INITIATED STUDY OF TEMPERATURE NOISE.
- PROVED NEUTRON NOISE CAN BE USED TO ASSESS BWR STABILITY AND APPLIED TO DRESDEN.
- COMPLETED STUDY OF SENSITIVITY OF PWR EX-CORE NEUTRON NOISE TO FUEL ELEMENT
  AND ISSUED DRAFT NUREG REPORT.
- ISSUED DRAFT NUREG REPORT OF LWR NEUTRON NOISE.
- ASSESSED A PRESSURE PERTURBATION METHOD TO MEASURE RESPONSE TIME.
