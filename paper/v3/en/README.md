# V3 English manuscript mirror

This directory is the English mirror of the Chinese source manuscript in `../zh/`. It has the same section numbering but is not an independent source of claims.

Translate a section only after its Chinese counterpart has been checked for factual support, scope, and engineering boundary. Do not introduce results, causal explanations, or claims that are absent from the Chinese source.

- `main_en.md`: English manuscript entry point.
- `sections/`: section-by-section English mirrors.
