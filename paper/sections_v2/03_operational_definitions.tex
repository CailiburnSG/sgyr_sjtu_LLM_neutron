
\subsection{Operational definitions and auditable interface}
\label{sec:operational-definitions}
Let $\mathbf{X}=\{x_{t,c}\}$ denote a chronologically sorted multichannel record, where $t$ indexes the sample and $c$ indexes the detector channel. The system does not ask the planner to estimate these quantities. Deterministic probes compute them and write their results to the workspace blackboard; the LLM only selects registered probes and organizes the resulting evidence.

For each channel, the anomaly probe uses a centered rolling window $W_t$ of 31 samples. Its local baseline and robust local deviation are
\begin{equation}
m_{t,c}=\operatorname{median}_{\tau\in W_t}x_{\tau,c}, \qquad
d_{t,c}=\operatorname{median}_{\tau\in W_t}\left|x_{\tau,c}-m_{\tau,c}\right|.
\label{eq:rolling-mad}
\end{equation}
The implementation marks a sample as a spike candidate only when both a local and a global-amplitude condition hold:
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
I^{\mathrm{spike}}_{t,c}&=
\mathbb{I}\left(\left|x_{t,c}-m_{t,c}\right|>5d_{t,c}\right)
\mathbb{I}\left(\left|x_{t,c}-m_{t,c}\right|>0.05R_c\right),\\
R_c&=\max_t x_{t,c}-\min_t x_{t,c}.
\end{aligned}
\label{eq:spike-rule}
\end{equation}
Contiguous positive samples are merged into one spike episode. An isolated zero event is separately recorded by $I^{\mathrm{zero}}_{t,c}=\mathbb{I}(x_{t,c}=0)$. These rules define detected signal events, not physical fault labels.

For a channel pair $(i,j)$, the discovery probe computes Pearson correlation
\begin{equation}
r_{ij}=\frac{\sum_t(x_{t,i}-\bar{x}_i)(x_{t,j}-\bar{x}_j)}
{\sqrt{\sum_t(x_{t,i}-\bar{x}_i)^2}\sqrt{\sum_t(x_{t,j}-\bar{x}_j)^2}}.
\label{eq:pearson}
\end{equation}
The current implementation flags near-redundancy at $r_{ij}\geq0.99$ and strong co-movement for $0.85\leq r_{ij}<0.99$. Together with lag, extremes, and spatial-snapshot outputs, these quantities form a structured alert interface
\begin{equation}
\mathbf{a}=g(\mathbf{X})=\{\mathbf{a}_{\mathrm{health}},\mathbf{a}_{\mathrm{morph}},
\mathbf{a}_{\mathrm{anom}},\mathbf{a}_{\mathrm{lag}},\mathbf{a}_{\mathrm{spatial}}\},
\label{eq:alert-summary}
\end{equation}
which is passed to retrieval. This separation prevents free-form text from becoming the sole interface between measurement analysis and manual-grounded explanation.
